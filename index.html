Tournament Management

Run & Join Events on PickleRankPH

From club ladders to national qualifiers — one platform for registration, brackets, live scoring, and official PR Rating updates. No more spreadsheets, group chats, or manual seeding.

PickleRankPH tournament management is built for Philippine clubs and organizers who want a professional player experience: discoverable events, verified results, and rankings that actually move when matches are confirmed.

  • Online registration

    Divisions by format, skill bracket, PR Rating range, or national rank — with capacity and entry fees.

  • Live brackets & courts

    Single/double elimination, round robin, pool play, and more — with court assignment and match command center.

  • Verified results → ratings

    Confirmed scores feed the PickleRanking System so every match can count toward national standings.

  • Club-hosted workflows

    Approve entries, manage hosted draws, and run internal leagues alongside open public events.
